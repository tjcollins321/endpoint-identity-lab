# When you leave

What happens to your account, your files, and your devices when you leave, and what you should do
before then.

## Before your last day

- Move anything the team needs out of personal folders into shared drives or shared folders; your
  Drive files will be handed to your manager, but a tidy handover is kinder.
- Return every company device (Mac, Chromebook, iPad) to IT.
- Take copies of personal items you keep in work systems (for example, a personal contact list);
  after the last day the account is not reachable.

## On the last day

Access ends all at once: sessions are signed out, every app that was authorized to your account is
disconnected, and the account is suspended. Mail sent to you is retained but not delivered to you, and a copy of new mail goes to your manager so work does not stall; nobody reads your old mailbox.
If you added the work account to a personal phone, it is removed from the phone, along with the
work mail and calendar on it; nothing personal is touched.

## After

- Ownership of your Drive files and calendars passes to your manager.
- A company Mac or Chromebook that has not been returned is locked remotely and shows a message to
  return it.
- The account is kept, suspended, for thirty days, then deleted. Mail may be retained longer for
  record-keeping, in archive, not in a mailbox anyone reads day to day.

## Questions

Ask IT or your manager before your last day; after it, the manager is the contact.
