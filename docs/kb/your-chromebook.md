# Your Chromebook

The Chromebook you were handed is enrolled in the company's device management before you sign in,
and it configures itself from your work account. There is nothing to install.

## First sign-in

Open the lid and sign in with your work address and password; set up 2-Step Verification if you
have not yet ([Your first day](first-day-setup.md)). The sign-in screen says "managed by
tjcollins.dev", which is the enrollment. Your mail, calendar, files, and the browser settings for
your role apply within a minute.

## What is set on the device

Company settings apply to the device whoever signs in (for example, only company accounts can sign
in, no guest mode, and the device re-enrolls itself if it is ever reset), and user settings apply
to you wherever you sign in. You can see the full list at `chrome://policy`.

## What the company can see and do

It can see the device model, its version, who used it, and that it meets its settings; it can
disable the device remotely if it is lost or when you leave, which shows a return message on the
screen, and it can reset it to issue it to someone else. It does not read your files or browsing.

## Help

If the screen says the device is disabled, it is waiting to be returned; contact IT. For anything
else, ask IT or your manager.
