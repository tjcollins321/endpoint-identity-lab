# Working from your own computer

As a contractor you use your own computer. The company does not issue one and does not install
anything on yours. This is what is expected and what the company controls.

## Use Chrome, signed in with your work account

Install Google Chrome if you do not have it, and sign in to Chrome with your work address (the
profile icon at the top right). That profile is where work happens: it receives the company's
browser settings, keeps work bookmarks and passwords apart from your own, and signs out of the
work account after twelve hours, after which you sign in again. Keep personal browsing in your own
profile.

## What the company controls, and what it does not

- It controls the work account: who can sign in, for how long, which files can be shared with whom,
  and it can end access at any moment. Drive sharing with people outside the company follows the
  company's contractor rules.
- It does not manage your computer: no agent, no profile, no visibility of your files, other
  accounts, or browsing outside the work profile, and no ability to lock or erase your machine.

## Expectations

Keep your computer's operating system current, its disk encrypted, and a screen lock on. The
company may ask you to confirm this. Never download company files outside the work profile.

## When the contract ends

Access to the work account ends on the agreed date; the work profile in Chrome stops working. Sign
out of it and remove it; nothing of yours is affected. See [When you leave](leaving.md).
