# Help articles

Written for the person, not the administrator: what to do on the first day, how to put the work
account on a personal phone, what a company Mac or Chromebook does on its own, what working from
your own computer means, and what happens when you leave. The welcome kit (`lifecycle/templates/`)
links to them; the administrator's side of each is in `runbooks/`. The phone article describes the
flow as designed: the lab has no personal device enrolled, which the top-level README lists under
what is not built.

| Article | Read it when |
|---|---|
| [Your first day](first-day-setup.md) | you have your work address and the password message |
| [Your work account on your own phone](personal-phone-work-account.md) | you want work mail and calendar on a personal phone |
| [Your company Mac](your-company-mac.md) | you have been issued a Mac |
| [Your Chromebook](your-chromebook.md) | you have been issued a Chromebook |
| [Working from your own computer](working-from-your-own-computer.md) | you are a contractor using your own machine |
| [When you leave](leaving.md) | you are leaving, or want to know what happens to your account |
