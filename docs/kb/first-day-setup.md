# Your first day

You have a work address ending in `@tjcollins.dev` and, in a separate message, a temporary
password. Here is what to do, in order. It takes about ten minutes.

## 1. Sign in and choose a password

Go to https://workspace.google.com, sign in with your work address and the temporary password, and
choose a new one when asked: at least 12 characters, something you do not use anywhere else. A
passphrase of a few unrelated words works well. You will not be asked to change it on a schedule.

If sign-in shows an extra verification step or says the account needs attention, follow it; a new
account sometimes needs one web sign-in before everything is unlocked. If it asks you to contact
your administrator, do.

## 2. Set up 2-Step Verification, today

Within one day of this first sign-in you must add a second step to your sign-in. Go to
https://myaccount.google.com/security, 2-Step Verification, and choose one of:

- **A passkey** on the device you are using (Touch ID, Face ID, or your computer's screen lock).
  Quickest, and nothing to type.
- **An authenticator app** (Google Authenticator or any TOTP app) on your phone.

Text messages and phone calls are not offered, on purpose: they can be intercepted. Add a second
method if you can, so losing a device does not lock you out. Until the step is set up, sign-in
may keep asking you for it; after the day is over, it will refuse.

## 3. Your mail, calendar, and files

Gmail, Calendar, and Drive are ready at the same address. You are already on the lists your role
uses (for example the company-wide list and your department's). If something you expected is
missing, ask your manager; access is granted by role, so a missing item is a quick fix.

## 4. Your device

Your welcome message says whether a Mac or a Chromebook is being prepared for you, or whether you
work from your own computer. See the matching article: [Your company Mac](your-company-mac.md),
[Your Chromebook](your-chromebook.md), or [Working from your own computer](working-from-your-own-computer.md).

## 5. Optional: your phone

Work mail and calendar on your personal phone: [Your work account on your own phone](personal-phone-work-account.md).

## If something goes wrong

Reply to the welcome message or ask your manager. Never share your password with anyone, including
IT; nobody who works here will ask for it.
